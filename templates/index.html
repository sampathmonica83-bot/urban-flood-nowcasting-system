Suggested workflow:

  1. Monitor rainfall and water-level feeds.
  2. Check high-risk locations on the map.
  3. Review citizen complaints.
  4. Verify reports with field teams or sensors.
  5. Issue official warnings through authorized channels.

Important: This academic prototype does not automatically issue government orders or emergency alerts.

🛟 Flood Safety

  • Do not walk or drive through moving or unknown-depth floodwater.
  • Stay away from open drains, manholes and electrical equipment.
  • Move to higher ground if local authorities issue an evacuation warning.
  • Keep essential medicines, water, documents and a charged phone ready.
  • For an immediate emergency, contact your local emergency services.
